- Created Kamloops FBP Priority Mapping from the UBC-FRESH dev workflow seed. The agent contract, roadmap, docs skeleton, and validator came from the seed. Every phase is planned, and no parent issues exist yet.
- Started the Phase 0 feasibility speed run on `feature/p0-bootstrap`. The local repository is seeded and the project contract, roadmap, README, ADRs, mental map, and data audit were rewritten for the KFC project.
- Fetched the KFC boundary, BEC map, provincial FBP fuel types, WUI buffer, and WUI risk class through `femic`'s documented BC Data Catalogue paths. The KFC is 71,579.2 km²; the three test zones cover 34,778 km² (ICH 13,324; IDF 18,504; PP 2,950). The current BEC map has 13 zones with non-zero KFC area, not the 16 in the proposal.
- Fetched 80 ClimateBC 2014–2023 July/August GeoTIFFs and 24 Copernicus GLO-30 DEM tiles. ClimateBC's files carry no CRS tag, and its published variable list has no wind speed or direction. The prototype therefore uses a documented fixed 20 km/h wind from 225° and a 62-day mean-summer spin-up; this is ADR 0002, not an observation.
- Downloaded and unpacked the 2.48 GB provincial fuel-type FGDB. The KFC bounding box contains 1,388,353 polygons, including modifier-prefixed codes such as `B26_O-1a/b` and `B71_S-2`. The crosswalk normalizes the prefix before mapping to the cffdrs raster codes.
- Measured LidarBC coverage five ways. Project extents cover 45.7% of the KFC; point-cloud tiles 26.8%; DEM 1:20k 30.0%; DSM 1:2.5k 19.1%; DSM 1:20k 17.0%. None reproduces the proposal's 54% claim. In the three test zones, point-cloud coverage is 19.2% (ICH), 31.2% (IDF), and 58.8% (PP).
- Built the 100 m EPSG:3005 analysis grid, KFC mask, BEC/fuel/WUI rasters, terrain (slope p50/p90/p99 = 19.7/60.7/95.5%), and the 100 m FBP input stack. The full KFC has 7,157,982 cells; the three zones have 3,477,490.
- Ran the official `cffdrs` 1.9.2 `fwiRaster` over a 62-day 4 km mean-summer sequence. The final codes are FFMC 56.6–88.0 (mean 81.0), BUI 1.4–209.1 (mean 119.8), and FWI 0.3–35.9 (mean 18.1).
- The 100 m full-rectangle `fbpRaster` run was stopped because it processes the 17.2 M-cell rectangle, including 10 M outside-KFC cells. The run was replaced by a 200 m full-KFC overview and a 100 m three-zone stack, which is the actual method comparison.
- Completed the 200 m full-KFC FBP run in 0.97 minutes and the 100 m three-zone FBP run in 3.73 minutes, both with 8 R workers. The first 100 m three-zone output was invalid because `fbpRaster` recycled values across NaN-filled cells; the input was refilled with valid non-fuel values outside the three zones and the run was repeated.
- Fixed two output bugs found by inspection. `terra::writeRaster` had written every FBP layer as uint32, truncating CFB and writing FD with a 4294967295 nodata sentinel; `run_fbp.R` now writes `FLT4S` with `NAflag=NaN`. The map helper had passed `(minx, miny, maxx, maxy)` to `imshow`, which expects `(left, right, bottom, top)`, so the rasters were flipped relative to the KFC boundary; the extent is fixed.
- Baseline result, 100 m three-zone method test: IDF HFI mean 1,828 kW/m (p90 3,013; p99 21,061), ROS mean 4.99 m/min, flame mean 2.00 m; ICH HFI mean 697 (p90 1,674), ROS 1.17 m/min; PP HFI mean 1,562 (p90 2,943), ROS 7.46 m/min. Under the mean-summer scenario, surface fire dominates and crown fire is rare at 100 m.
- Resolution sensitivity is material: the 200 m full-KFC run gives IDF HFI mean 1,972 and 4.2% crown fire, versus 1,828 and 0.1% at 100 m; PP moves from 0.0% crown at 100 m to 4.8% at 200 m. The thesis must treat resolution as a sensitivity axis.
